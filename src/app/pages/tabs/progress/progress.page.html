📉 What is "Weight Trend"?

Your body weight naturally fluctuates daily due to water retention, sodium, and digestion.

Daily shows your raw daily weight. Trend filters out this daily noise to show your true physical progress.

How it works: We use an exponential smoothing algorithm famously detailed in The Hacker's Diet. It dampens sudden daily spikes so you can focus on long-term body tissue changes, not temporary water weight.
